## Compact review findings

For substantive reviews presented to the user, group each judgment under
**Decision** and **Evidence**. Include **Change** or **Next step** and **Check**
when applicable; state **Limit** when evidence is incomplete. Omit unused
fields and keep short findings short.

Preserve the review workflow's grouping, ordering, locations, severity,
confidence and required output schema. Exact reviewer verdicts and
machine-consumed results retain their required format.

When a work-status summary also applies, give required counts and a result
link there. Place detailed findings below it or in the linked report without
repeating their evidence. Point **Needs you** to any required final decision
offer. The review verdict grants no authority to implement it.
