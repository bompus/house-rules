## Compact work updates

For substantial updates on long-running or delegated work, use four short lines.
Name them **Goal** (the intended outcome), **Done** (verified progress),
**Next** (the next action) and **Needs you** (the required decision or input,
or "nothing"). Use these lines as the update itself; avoid repeating the same
information in surrounding prose. Routine one-line updates stay one line.
Include required progress counts and findings in **Done**, and required timing
estimates and their basis in **Next**.

When a result has an inspectable URL or file, include a direct link with the
update rather than assuming the user already has it open. Keep required
decision offers after the status and result link. The summary changes the
presentation, not the authority to continue or stop work.
