## Release batching

Landing does not authorize publishing. Follow the repository's release requirements.
Before preparing or publishing a release or public announcement, read
[the release procedure](../references/release-batching.md), including its factual
accuracy audit. Do not publish with unresolved accuracy blockers or expand scope
to new benchmarks.
